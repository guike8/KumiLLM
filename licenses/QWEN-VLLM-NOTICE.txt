KumiLLM modifications:
Schema-aware scalar matching and XML argument conversion were changed to
support anyOf, oneOf and type-array unions. Unambiguously numeric values keep
their number type; ambiguous string branches and the -inf sentinel stay text.
Invalid or uncertain conversions stay text for downstream validation. Focused
regression checks and a reversible, version/hash-guarded installer were added.
